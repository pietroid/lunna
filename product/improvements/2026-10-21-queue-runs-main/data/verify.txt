Closes #14 · `research` · **RFC ready**, waiting for your reply on #14.

I looked at Hermes Agent and OpenClaw and updated the RFC. The recommendation hasn't changed. In both, a chat gateway sits in front of an agent of their own, and that agent calls `claude -p`, the same command the queue already runs. Anthropic's terms keep your subscription for Claude Code itself, so that agent would need a second bill with per-token API pricing. Neither has gates, PRs or a budget, and each is a long-running gateway you'd have to keep patched (OpenClaw had a one-click token-theft CVE in 2026).

Your question and its answer are under "Why not a third-party console". The evidence is in a new section, "Hermes, OpenClaw and other agent gateways", and both tools have rows in data/options.md. I added idea 5 and a matching ledger entry: "The harness that holds the login holds the bill". Look at decision 6: trying Hermes on Telegram with a cheap model in front of `claude -p`. I recommend not now, since Remote Control already gives you a chat from the phone at no extra cost.
